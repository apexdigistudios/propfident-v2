# PropFident

> AI Context File: This document provides LLMs and AI agents with a comprehensive understanding of PropFident, its goals, architecture, and feature set.

## Overview
PropFident (propfident.online) is a SaaS platform designed for proprietary firm traders. It provides real-time drawdown monitoring, account matching, and advanced risk management tools to ensure traders never breach their funded accounts.

## Our Mission & Goals
The core goal of PropFident is to solve the highest cause of failure in the prop firm space: account breaches due to poor risk management and misunderstood drawdown rules. 

Our primary objectives are to:
- **Protect Funded Accounts:** Act as a safeguard for traders, preventing emotional or math-based errors from causing an account loss.
- **Demystify Drawdown Rules:** Provide absolute clarity on complex trailing and static drawdown rules across different prop firms.
- **Automate Risk Management:** Calculate lot sizing and risk buffers automatically, allowing traders to focus purely on market execution.

## How Everything Works
PropFident acts as a bridge between the trader's terminal and their prop firm's specific rule set.

1. **Terminal Integration:** The platform uses MetaApi to securely connect and synchronize live account data, equity fluctuations, and automated trade history directly from MetaTrader 4 (MT4) and MetaTrader 5 (MT5).
2. **Real-Time Drawdown Monitoring:** The system continuously tracks open floating equity against closed balance. It calculates the exact monetary distance to both the daily loss limit and the maximum overall drawdown limit.
3. **Firm-Specific Rule Enforcement:** Because every prop firm has different rules, PropFident's logic adapts to calculate based on End-of-Day (EOD) static drawdowns, high-water mark trailing drawdowns, or fixed absolute drawdowns.
4. **Risk Buffer Calculation:** As trades progress, the system dynamically recalculates the trader's "profit buffer" and maximum allowable position sizing for the next trade to mathematically prevent a breach.

## Core Platform Features
- **Live MT4/MT5 Dashboard:** A centralized hub for live equity tracking and automated trade journaling.
- **Dynamic Position Sizing:** Automatically outputs the safe lot size based on current available daily drawdown.
- **Prop Firm Matching:** Advanced matching logic that pairs a trader's specific style (e.g., swing trading, high win-rate, scalping) with the proprietary firm rules that best suit them.

## Free Tools
We provide a suite of un-gated, interactive tools for the community to utilize without an account:
- **Drawdown Simulator:** Allows users to input a starting balance and simulate win/loss streaks to visualize how close they would get to breaching specific prop firm limits.
- **Position Size & Risk Calculator:** A straightforward tool to calculate exact lot sizes across various forex pairs and asset classes based on a percentage of the account.
- **Rule Comparison Engine:** A database outlining and comparing the specific static and trailing drawdown rules of top firms (e.g., FTMO, FundedNext, Funding Pips, Topstep).
